# Swing and putting film

## Angles

1. **Down-the-line** — on the target line, hand/hip height. Plane, path, shaft
   at the top, **trail elbow in-and-down (the slot)**. RED = slot + shaft plane.
2. **Face-on** — chest height, square. Posture, weight, hip clearance, low point.
3. Overhead — putting only.

Slo-mo 240fps when the phone will. Three swings per angle.

## One day, one session

All clips that day → one `session`. Later files → `session-update`. Check the
lab's film room first so a re-send is not a second session.

## Red marks on a frame

At most three, in red:

- Target line / shaft plane
- Trail-arm slot (in and down)
- The one fault this clip actually shows

Do not red-mark every joint.

## Feel vs film

Jack names the thought ("dropping the trail hand"). The film says whether the
slot actually happened. Quote both. The film wins on the position.
